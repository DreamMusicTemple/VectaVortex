# Interstellar - spectral particle ambient FX stage

FX type `interstellar`, available on every layer (web, JUCE, AUv3). Any incoming audio is analysed, split into six bands, and re-synthesised
as evolving pads: pitched particles (separate Pitch Up / Pitch Down, snapped to the detected key), periodic chop-and-loop of the extracted
harmonic material (stretched and resynthesised), harmonic resonators that follow each segment's own harmony, automatic Evolve / Drift
movement and a built-in reverb (size, decay, damping, modulation, width, shimmer, freeze).

* Source: **Live** (rolling 1-16 s buffer, Freeze) or **Capture** (CAPTURE NOW, auto-captures on first use).
* Quality: Eco / Normal / Lush (FFT 2048 / 4096 / 8192; 10/20/36 particles; 6/12/16 resonators).
* Mix is dry/wet, 100% wet by default. Character macro: neutral > shimmer > dark > choir > glass.
* 24 presets: `Assets/Interstellar/presets.json` (plus full patches `Assets/Presets/INTERSTELLAR - *`). Generated by `tools/gen_interstellar_presets.py`.
* Modulation: every continuous dial is a destination `FXP:interstellar:<key>` (39 destinations, plus freeze / revFreeze), from any source
  (LFOs, envelopes, macros, MIDI, XY pads...). `captureLen` and the discrete selectors are not destinations.
